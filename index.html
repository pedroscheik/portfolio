About

Strategy first, technology as the lever

I've spent the last decade figuring out how businesses actually work — not on paper, but in practice. I started out mapping processes on the ground, at a shared services center, learning what breaks when a handoff between teams isn't designed carefully. That early, hands-on view of operations is still the lens I bring to every strategic conversation I'm part of today.

These days I lead digital and business transformation initiatives — the kind that start as a slide in a strategy deck and end as something hundreds of people actually use. I care about getting the strategy right, but I care just as much about the messy part in between: aligning stakeholders, driving the change, and making sure adoption isn't left to chance. That combination is what let me contribute to a B2B expansion that generated ~R$24M in its first year, and to a competency program that changed how ~90 people were developed.

I'm currently pursuing an MBA in Management & Digital Transformation at Universidade de São Paulo, and I'm looking for a remote role where I can operate at that same intersection — close enough to strategy to help shape direction, close enough to execution to make sure it actually happens. I've presented strategy in English to audiences of 400+, and I'm comfortable working async with teams in the US and Europe.
